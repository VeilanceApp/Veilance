Privacy basics

A quick guide to this report

You do not need to be a privacy expert. These four ideas explain most of what you see here.

Request

A small job the browser performs to load a file or exchange data. Many requests are normal.

Outside service

A domain different from the website you opened. It may provide a useful feature or track activity.

Known tracker

A service associated with analytics or advertising. A match is evidence to inspect, not proof of wrongdoing.

Fingerprinting

Combining browser traits to recognize a device. Shield can change supported traits; it does not block every request.
