Privacy

Review the privacy policy.

Monitoring and history stay in this browser by default. Accepting the policy does not turn on telemetry.

Veilance Privacy Policyveilance.org/privacy ↗
  • Visits stay local

    Complete visit sessions are stored only in this browser and limited to the 20 most recent visits.

  • Uploads require a separate choice

    Accepting the policy does not enable telemetry. You decide that on the next screen.

  • Private keys stay private

    The locally generated Solana wallet keypair is not synced or sent to Veilance.

Optional telemetry

Choose whether to share telemetry.

Telemetry is off unless you enable it. This choice does not affect local monitoring or Veilance Shield.

Choose a telemetry setting
VLNC
How telemetry rewards work

Accepted contributions may receive VLNC.

Rewards are sent to the public Solana wallet generated in this browser. Eligibility and payout amount depend on review; an upload does not guarantee payment. Back up the wallet in Settings before relying on it.

Performance note: automatic snapshots inspect page structure after an eligible visit. Complex pages or several tabs loading together may experience added latency. Turn the setting off if browsing feels slower.

Exactly what an upload includes
Sent

Exact website hostname, pseudonymous contributor ID, public wallet address, public IP address, timestamps, request and signal counts, tracker matches, page-structure evidence, and redacted HTML.

Never sent in a snapshot

Page text, URL paths or searches, form values, cookies, storage contents, authorization data, raw fingerprint values, private or internal hosts, and the wallet private key.
